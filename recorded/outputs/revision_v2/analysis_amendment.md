This analysis-source amendment adds supplementary world-model behavior-cloning comparisons before execution or inspection of the revised control-test matrix. It preserves every method and contrast in the original 28-method plan. The experiment owner records the matching method additions and timing in protocol_amendment.json; this note does not substitute for that protocol record.

The amended matrix has 34 methods. WM_BC_vision and WM_BC_visuotactile each contain fixed training seeds 0, 1, and 2, using the corresponding world model and the existing rl/<variant>_seed<s>/bc_actor.pt checkpoint. Both supplementary groups must be present in full when either is included. The analyzer also continues to accept the unchanged original 28-method matrix.

Three supplementary contrasts are appended: WM_vision minus WM_BC_vision; WM_visuotactile minus WM_BC_visuotactile; and WM_BC_visuotactile minus BC_visuotactile. The first two compare subsequent policy improvement against the behavior-cloning actor on the same learned world-model representation, including the extra optimization budget. The third compares representation and training pipelines with distinct training budgets; it cannot isolate a representation effect.

The environment sets, metrics, 2,000 paired environment-bootstrap replicates, seed 20401010, and separate reporting of policy-training-seed variation remain unchanged. All intervals remain pointwise with no multiplicity correction. The input amendment file is hashed in the output provenance when present. Synthetic validation covers the complete 34-by-120 matrix, backward compatibility with the original matrix, and rejection of an incomplete supplementary group set.
